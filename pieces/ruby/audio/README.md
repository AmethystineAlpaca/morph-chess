Each new break randomly selects one of these recordings. Their PCM samples are unchanged; non-audio container metadata has been removed.

- Author: BristolStories.

- Duration: 1.92 seconds; stereo PCM, 44.1 kHz, 16 bit.
- Playback gain: 0.7.

- Original filename: `199906__gn2013__breaking-glass-stereo.wav`
- Local asset: `breaking-glass-stereo.wav`
- Author: GN2013.
- Source: https://freesound.org/people/GN2013/sounds/199906/
- License: [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- Duration: 2.048889 seconds; stereo PCM, 44.1 kHz, 16 bit.
- Playback gain: 0.9, to bring its quieter attack closer to the first recording.

The recording starts at the visual fracture, 0.65 seconds into the animation.
Playback speed follows the animation, including the pitch change in slow motion.
Pausing, resuming, scrubbing and changing speed retain the selected recording.
If one recording cannot load, the remaining recording is still usable.

The README's recorded demo includes these glass effects and carries the same sound credits and applicable license restrictions. The silent GIFs contain no audio.
